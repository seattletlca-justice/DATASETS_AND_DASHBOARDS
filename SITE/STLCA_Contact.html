About This Site

STLCA is an independent data analysis organization. This website presents analysis of publicly available SDCI enforcement records — 233,543 code compliance records spanning 2003–2025.

STLCA is not a government agency. It does not accept complaints, provide legal advice, or represent individual tenants or landlords.
