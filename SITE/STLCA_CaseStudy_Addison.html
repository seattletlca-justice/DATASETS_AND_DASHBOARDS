Section 01 — The Property Record

What SDCI Records Show

STLCA compiled the full SDCI complaint and violation history for 308 4th Ave S using publicly available enforcement data. The record documents a pattern of code complaints that were resolved without documented on-site inspections — a closure method that STLCA's citywide analysis found accounts for the majority of eviction and habitability complaints across Seattle.

At The Addison on 4th, this pattern was present in the complaint record prior to the fire incident. Open violations and unresolved complaints were on file with SDCI at the time of the incident.

  • Complaint suppression post-fire: STLCA identified evidence that complaint activity following the fire incident was not fully reflected in publicly accessible records in a manner consistent with standard enforcement timelines.
  • Uninspected closures: Consistent with citywide patterns, multiple complaints at this property were closed without documented site visits.
  • NOV record compiled: A Notice of Violation record for this property has been compiled as part of STLCA's document package.

Section 02 — Context

Part of a Citywide Pattern

The Addison case is one of several third-party property cases STLCA has documented as part of its analysis of SDCI enforcement systemics. Across all seven Council districts, STLCA found that eviction-related complaints are closed without documented inspections at rates exceeding 85%, peaking at 98.8% in 2021.

The Addison on 4th represents a case where those enforcement gaps had direct safety consequences. STLCA's full report will present the complete timeline and documentary record.
