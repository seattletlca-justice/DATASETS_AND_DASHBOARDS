SUBJECT: Housing Enforcement Data for Your District — STLCA Briefing

Council Member [NAME],

The Seattle Tenant Landlord Compliance Authority has compiled an independent
analysis of 233,543 SDCI housing enforcement records (2003–2025). We found
that 59.8% of landlord-tenant cases closed with no documented inspection
result — and that Ordinance 125054, which required SDCI to report annually
to Council beginning in 2017, has never been fulfilled in nine years.

We have prepared a one-page briefing for your review, along with a full
per-district dashboard showing the enforcement record for your constituents:
